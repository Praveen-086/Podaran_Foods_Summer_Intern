# Podaran Foods – Summer Internship 2025

## 📌 About the Internship

This repository documents my 45-day Summer Institutional Training completed at **Podaran Foods India Private Limited**, as part of the **M.Com (Finance and Computer Applications)** programme at **Bharathiar University, Coimbatore**.

The internship provided practical exposure to beverage manufacturing operations, production workflows, manufacturing processes, quality control, packaging, workplace hygiene, machine coordination, and industrial process management.

# ⚙️ Production Department

The Production Department is responsible for preparing the beverage base before it moves to the subsequent manufacturing stages.

### Major activities observed

- Receipt of raw materials
- Storage and sorting
- Raw material preparation
- Ingredient measurement
- Mixing
- Filtration
- Cooling
- Transfer to storage tanks

### Raw materials observed

Examples include:

- Fruit pulp
- Sugar
- Flavouring essence
- Citric acid
- RO water
- Preservatives

### Production process

```text
Raw Material Receipt
        ↓
Storage & Sorting
        ↓
Ingredient Preparation
        ↓
Mixing
        ↓
Filtration
        ↓
Cooling
        ↓
Chilled Storage
        ↓
Transfer to Manufacturing
```

---

# 🏗️ Manufacturing Department

The Manufacturing Department is responsible for converting the prepared beverage material into a finished bottled product. During the internship, I observed bottle blowing, bottle cooling, beverage filling, conveyor movement, and machine monitoring.

### Major activities observed

- PET preform handling
- Bottle blowing
- Bottle cooling
- Beverage filling
- Conveyor movement
- Machine monitoring
- Initial production-line checks

### Manufacturing Process

```text
PET Preform
      ↓
Heating
      ↓
Blow Moulding
      ↓
Bottle Formation
      ↓
Bottle Cooling
      ↓
Beverage Filling
      ↓
Conveyor Transfer
      ↓
Machine Monitoring
```

---

# 🔍 Quality Control Department

The Quality Control (QC) Department plays an important role in ensuring that beverages are safe, consistent, properly filled, correctly labelled, and suitable for distribution.

### Major Quality Checks

- Filtration checks
- Bottle volume verification
- Bottle appearance inspection
- Cap inspection
- Leakage detection
- Product appearance checks
- Label verification
- Batch number verification
- Manufacturing date verification
- MRP verification
- Use-by / expiry date verification

### Quality Control Process

```text
Filtration Check
       ↓
Bottle Filling
       ↓
Volume Verification
       ↓
Bottle Inspection
       ↓
Cap & Leakage Check
       ↓
Label Verification
       ↓
Date & MRP Verification
       ↓
Defect Detection
       ↓
Accepted / Rejected Product
```

# 📦 Packaging Department

The Packaging Department prepares the finished beverage products for storage, transportation, and distribution. The process combines automated machinery with manual supervision to maintain packaging accuracy and product presentation.

### Major Activities Observed

- Bottle transfer
- Labelling
- Batch and date printing
- MRP printing
- Defect detection
- Shrink wrapping
- Pallet stacking
- Warehouse transfer

### Packaging Process

```text

          ↓
Labelling

Date & MRP Printing
          ↓
Defect Detection
          ↓
Shrink Wrapping
          ↓
Pallet Stacking
          ↓
Warehouse
          ↓
Dispatch
```
